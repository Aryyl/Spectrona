Overview & Philosophy

About Jeev One

Calm, continuous bedside intelligence crafted around everyday human dignity and local privacy.

Jeev One was created with a clear purpose: to make continuous health monitoring comfortable, seamless, and deeply reliable.

Combining wearable physiological sensing with local edge computing, our team is driven by a passion for everyday human care and true data sovereignty.

What we do?

We provide continuous vital intelligence with a personal, offline-first touch.

Our Sensors

Our wearable sensing stack captures multi-parameter biometrics including Lead-I electrical ECG via AD8232, optical SpO₂ via MAX30102, and non-contact core temperature via MLX90614 — calibrated for effortless daily wear.

To make monitoring even more meaningful, ambient room temperature and humidity via DHT22 combine with periodic UART blood pressure intervals so caregivers gain complete environmental and physiological context.

Accessibility & Privacy

At Jeev One, autonomy and privacy are at the heart of what we do. We believe health awareness should be effortless and dignified for everyone.

Built especially for older adults living independently and post-operative recovery, every measurement is validated locally on the Raspberry Pi 5 and preserved in private SQLite WAL storage without cloud dependency.

Our Edge Intelligence

Our ESP32 microcontroller and Raspberry Pi 5 gateway aren’t just simple data relays — they execute deterministic tri-domain risk scoring and adaptive EMA baseline calibration in real time.

Whether connected across a home network or running in disconnected environments, vital trends stay accurate, auditable, and reassuring for families and clinicians alike.

Embedded Engineering

ESP32 & Raspberry Pi 5 Stack

How microcontroller scheduling on the ESP32 and local edge validation on the Raspberry Pi 5 convert raw sensor voltages into auditable, zero-cloud clinical records.

01 // WEARABLE CONTROLLER

What We Have Done with the ESP32

The ESP32 serves as the wearable’s real-time acquisition and communication controller between physical transducers and the local Raspberry Pi 5 gateway:

  • Timed Polling Schedules duty-cycled sensor slots to preserve compact wearable battery life.
  • Payload Packaging Serializes synchronized multi-parameter readings into clean standardized JSON packets.
  • NTP Clock Synchronization Resolves cold-boot timestamp: 0 anomalies and publishes valid Unix epochs.
  • Wi-Fi MQTT Publisher Streams packets reliably to topic health/sensors with local retry handling.

02 // EDGE INTELLIGENCE GATEWAY

What We Have Done with the Raspberry Pi 5

The Raspberry Pi 5 acts as an autonomous local compute node — performing full validation, baseline adaptation, and risk fusion without cloud relays:

  • Mosquitto Broker High-throughput local MQTT messaging bus bound on port 1883 with zero cloud reliance.
  • Physiological Validation Verifies epoch timestamps, numeric types, and human anatomical bounds before commits.
  • Local SQLite WAL Database High-concurrency Write-Ahead Log retaining continuous local clinical telemetry histories.
  • Deterministic Risk Engine Computes vital, thermal, and respiratory strain against slow-EMA personal baselines.
  • Systemd Auto-Start Daemon Configured as an enabled system unit that recovers cleanly after any power cycle.

Deployment & Clinical Impact

Bridging embedded edge engineering with everyday human care — built for independent aging, chronic condition monitoring, and uncompromised patient privacy.

Primary Deployment Focus

Older Adults & Independent Living

Strongest initial fit. Designed for older adults living independently or in assisted living homes — capturing vital signs unobtrusively during daily routines while retaining private local records and notifying families when clinical drift occurs.

Clinician-Guided Expansion

Chronic Conditions & Post-Op Recovery

Well suited under physician oversight for patients managing hypertension, chronic respiratory recovery requiring longitudinal SpO₂ review, or structured post-discharge home monitoring without frequent clinic travel.

STEP 01

Passive Everyday Monitoring

The patient wears the compact ESP32 node. Sensors sample vitals in quiet, scheduled low-power intervals without interrupting daily life.

STEP 02

Raspberry Pi 5 Local Ingestion

Frames arrive over local Wi-Fi MQTT. The gateway verifies timestamps, commits SQLite WAL records, and evaluates tri-domain clinical strain.

STEP 03

Zero-Latency Live Dashboard

Clinicians and caregivers inspect live vitals, Lead-I waveforms, and longitudinal baselines directly over local LAN on desktop or mobile PWA.
