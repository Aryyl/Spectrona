The Complete System Architecture

The system combines wearable sensing, edge computing, and cloud monitoring into a robust, synchronized data pipeline.

STAGE 01 / WEARABLE SENSING
TRANSDUCER LAYER

Wearable Sensors

Collects multi-parameter biometric and environmental signals during everyday activities: heart rate/ECG, blood oxygen (SpO₂), body temperature, room temperature, and relative humidity.

• AD8232 ECG / HR Module • MAX30102 Optical SpO₂ • MLX90614 Infrared Temp • DHT22 Ambient Monitor
STAGE 02 / MICROCONTROLLER
DATA PACKETIZATION

ESP32 Controller

Reads connected sensors in scheduled timed slots, synchronizes its clock using NTP so readings have valid Unix timestamps, formats JSON packets, and publishes to MQTT over Wi-Fi.

• NTP Clock Synchronization • Scheduled Timed Polling • Wi-Fi MQTT Publisher • Topic: health/sensors
STAGE 03 / EDGE COMPUTING
VALIDATION & RISK ENGINE

Raspberry Pi 5 Gateway

Mosquitto broker receives MQTT messages on port 1883. The gateway validates timestamps, checks numeric ranges, stores records in SQLite with patient associations, derives composite risk scores, and manages the cloud sync outbox.

• Mosquitto MQTT Broker (1883) • Local SQLite Database Storage • Rule-Based Tri-Domain Risk • Systemd Background Daemon
STAGE 04 / CLOUD LAYER
CLOUD FIRESTORE

Firebase Cloud Storage

Stores synchronized sensor records and derived risk classifications in the patient_readings collection. Enables remote access from any location without opening local Pi ports.

• Collection: patient_readings • Timestamp & Patient ID Index • Outbox Retry Queue Sync • Secure Remote Accessibility
STAGE 05 / CLIENT ACCESS
PROGRESSIVE WEB APP

Web Dashboard (PWA)

Displays live real-time vitals, Lead-I ECG strip diagnostics, historical telemetry tables, dynamic baseline calibration, and alarm indicators to authorized caregivers and clinicians.

• Realtime onSnapshot Streaming • Mobile PWA Touch Optimized • Multi-Patient Profile Switching • Offline App Shell Resilience

What We Built on the ESP32 & Raspberry Pi 5

How our firmware and edge gateway turn raw electrical signals into validated, synchronized clinical records.

01 / WEARABLE CONTROLLER

What We Have Done with the ESP32

The ESP32 is the wearable's data-collection and communication controller between the sensors and the Raspberry Pi:

  • • Reads sensors in timed slots to conserve battery.
  • • Consolidates readings into a standardized JSON packet.
  • • NTP Clock Synchronization: Fixed the timestamp: 0 issue; now publishes valid Unix timestamps so the gateway accepts all readings.
  • • Connects over Wi-Fi MQTT: Publishes to health/sensors with verified successful transmission.
MQTT PAYLOAD • health/sensors VERIFIED FORMAT

  "device_id": "patient_01",
  "timestamp": 1792414171,
  "heart_rate": 75,
  "spo2": 97,
  "body_temperature": 36.5,
  "room_temperature": 27.3,
  "humidity": 52.1
}
02 / EDGE INTELLIGENCE GATEWAY

What We Have Done with the Raspberry Pi 5

The Raspberry Pi 5 acts as the local gateway and edge-computing unit—more than just a bridge to the cloud:

  • • MQTT Broker (Mosquitto): Receives sensor packets on port 1883 and subscribes to health/sensors.
  • • Validation & Plausibility: Validates timestamps, numeric types, and physiological limits before recording.
  • • Local SQLite Database: Stores historical readings and patient profiles (Patient 1 and Patient 2) completely offline.
  • • Rule-Based Risk Processing: Derives vital strain, heat stress, and respiratory risk indicators with recommendations.
  • • Background Cloud Sync: Outbox queue uploads eligible patient records to Firebase Firestore with automatic retries.
  • • Systemd Auto-Start Daemon: Configured as an enabled system service that boots automatically on power-up.

How Spectrona Can Help People in Daily Life

The value of a wearable health monitor is not simply collecting numbers. It is helping people and caregivers notice changes over time and make better-informed decisions.

CASE 01 // INDEPENDENT LIVING

Older Adults Living Independently

A caregiver can review recorded heart rate, oxygen saturation, and temperature trends remotely. Environmental readings provide critical context during hot weather.

Potential benefit: Greater visibility into day-to-day health trends without requiring a caregiver to be physically present for every measurement.
CASE 02 // CHRONIC CARE

People with Chronic Conditions

People with hypertension, respiratory conditions, or known cardiac conditions benefit from recording relevant measurements over time with clinician guidance.

Potential benefit: Organized longitudinal records that support clinical discussions and help identify trends that deserve medical attention.
CASE 03 // POST-OP CARE

Patients Recovering at Home

Under a clinician-directed care plan, healthcare teams can review selected readings remotely instead of relying exclusively on occasional manual reports.

Potential benefit: Easier access to longitudinal readings and earlier follow-up when concerning physiological changes are detected.
CASE 04 // INDUSTRIAL SAFETY

Workers in Hot Environments

Ambient temperature and humidity, combined with physiological heart rate and body temperature, provide context for environmental heat strain.

Potential benefit: Contextual information supporting heat-safety practices and timely hydration breaks. (Not a certified heatstroke prevention device).
CASE 05 // REMOTE CAREGIVERS

Family Caregivers & Community Healthcare

A cloud-connected dashboard lets authorized family members review readings and baseline shifts from anywhere in the world.

Potential benefit: Shared peace of mind and longitudinal visibility without depending on manual phone calls or handwritten logs.
⚠️
Important Clinical Boundary: These are intended use cases, not proof of clinical benefit. The current prototype is best described as a health-monitoring and data-logging platform, not a certified diagnostic or emergency-response system.

Who Spectrona is Built For — and Who It Is Not

We focus on practical, high-value deployment rather than claiming the system is suited for every clinical scenario.

RECOMMENDED INITIAL FOCUS

Older Adults & Home Health Monitoring

Strongest initial fit because the system is designed to collect selected vital measurements over time, retain a history, and make records available remotely to caregivers.

  • ● Older adults living alone or in assisted care
  • ● Regular daily health check-ins without hospital travel
  • ● Environmental heat-strain context during hot weather
SECONDARY EXPANSION GROUPS

Clinician-Guided Secondary Groups

Suitable following sensor validation and guidance from healthcare professionals:

  • ● People with hypertension (once cuff hardware is validated)
  • ● People with respiratory conditions (SpO₂ tracking)
  • ● People with cardiac conditions (validated ECG rhythms)
  • ● Patients recovering at home under a clinician care plan
  • ● People exposed to environmental heat during work
CLEAR CLINICAL BOUNDARIES

Who Should NOT Be the Primary Target Yet

Safety and integrity first: the prototype should not be used in scenarios requiring certified medical hardware:

  • ✕ Patients requiring continuous, clinically certified ICU ECG
  • ✕ Patients depending on the device for life-critical alerts
  • ✕ Patients requiring continuous emergency blood-pressure lines
  • ✕ Anyone using Spectrona as a substitute for emergency services

Spectrona: A Wearable IoT-Based Health Monitoring System

Spectrona is a wearable health-monitoring project that integrates physiological sensors, an ESP32 microcontroller, a Raspberry Pi 5 edge gateway, and Firebase cloud storage. The wearable collects selected measurements such as heart rate, SpO₂, body temperature, and environmental temperature and humidity. The ESP32 transmits timestamped readings over Wi-Fi using MQTT to the Raspberry Pi, which validates the incoming data, stores it locally, calculates monitoring risk indicators, and synchronizes eligible patient records to Firebase Firestore.

The cloud-connected dashboard makes readings and historical trends accessible to authorized caregivers and healthcare professionals from different locations. Spectrona primarily targets older adults, people who need regular health monitoring at home, and people with chronic conditions who may benefit from longitudinal measurements. It is a monitoring-support system, not a replacement for professional medical assessment or emergency care.
