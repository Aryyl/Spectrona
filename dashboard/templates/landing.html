// ARCHITECTURE & CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Extreme Resilience

Most modern health trackers tether your biological telemetry to proprietary cloud servers. JEEV_ONE inverts this paradigm by executing multi-parameter biomedical intelligence entirely at the edge.

01 / EDGE-NATIVE COMPUTING

100% Offline Autonomy

Zero dependency on external internet connections or third-party servers. All data ingestion, schema validation, normalization, and local SQLite persistence execute directly on the edge node.

● Operating during total network outages & disaster zones
02 / COMPOSITE RISK ENGINE

Tri-Domain Risk Fusion

Isolated metrics can be deceiving. JEEV_ONE concurrently evaluates Environmental Heat Stress (Heat Index & wet-bulb), Vital Signs (hypertension, tachycardia, bradycardia), and Respiratory Strain (hypoxia).

● Context-aware clinical heuristics & early warning alerts
03 / CONTINUOUS CALIBRATION

Dynamic Personal Baselines

Replaces static population averages with adaptive Exponential Moving Averages (EMA). The device learns the individual’s unique physiological resting state while filtering out short-term transient spikes.

● Zero false alert fatigue caused by single bad readings
// INTEGRATED SENSOR SUITE

Clinical & Environmental Telemetry

JEEV_ONE interfaces industrial and medical-grade biometric transducers with a high-throughput edge controller for uninterrupted telemetry acquisition.

PULSE OXIMETRY I2C BUS

MAX30102

Blood Oxygen (SpO₂) & Heart Rate

High-sensitivity optical biosensor featuring integrated dual-wavelength red and infrared LEDs. Captures peripheral capillary photoplethysmography (PPG) waveforms to detect hypoxia and cardiac frequency.

Range SpO₂ 50–100%
Protocol I2C (0x57)
Sample Rate Up to 400 Hz
THERMOMETRY I2C BUS

MLX90614

Medical-Grade Non-Contact Body Temp

Infrared thermopile thermometer providing instantaneous, non-invasive core physiological temperature assessment with factory calibration for clinical accuracy.

Accuracy ±0.2 °C
Target Range -70 to 380 °C
Resolution 0.02 °C
ENVIRONMENTAL 1-WIRE BUS

DHT22 (AM2302)

Ambient Temperature & Humidity

Calibrated digital relative humidity and temperature sensor. Supplies real-time ambient parameters to compute Heat Index, wet-bulb values, and environmental heat strain.

Humidity 0–100% RH
Temp Range -40 to 80 °C
Precision ±2% RH
HEMODYNAMICS UART SERIAL

BP Sensor TTL-V3

Digital Blood Pressure (Sys / Dia)

Direct TTL serial-based arterial blood pressure monitoring module. Captures Systolic and Diastolic pressure (mmHg) to diagnose hypertensive crisis and circulatory distress.

Baud Rate 115200 bps
Sys Range 50–250 mmHg
Dia Range 30–150 mmHg
CARDIAC ELECTROPHYSIOLOGY ANALOG BIO-AMP

AD8232 Sensor

Single-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Integrated biopotential signal conditioning module designed to extract, amplify, and filter biopotential electrical signals from cardiac rhythms in the presence of noisy motion artifacts.

CMRR 80 dB
Signal P-Q-R-S-T wave
Bandwidth 0.5 to 40 Hz
EDGE PROCESSING HOST COMPUTER

Raspberry Pi 5

Central Edge Server & AI Engine

Houses the local MQTT subscriber, SQLite database repository, Flask SSE streaming service, and autonomous risk fusion engines. Drives both the local 3.5" TFT display and network dashboard.

SoC BCM2712 Quad
Local Storage SQLite (WAL)
Stream SSE / WebSockets
